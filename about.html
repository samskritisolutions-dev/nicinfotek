Who We Are · Established 2004

People working with technology to deliver simplified innovation.

Headquartered in Tampa, Florida, NIC Infotek is a fast-growing global community of IT and engineering professionals helping Fortune 2000 and emerging enterprises reduce risk, time, and cost.

Corporate Overview

Built on earned trust and measurable IT outcomes.

Since 2004, NIC Infotek has delivered global capabilities and operational efficiencies across application development, system enhancements, enterprise ERP, engineering, and strategic workforce solutions.

Our Philosophy

“It’s about the people working with the technology.”

At NIC Infotek, we recognize that even the most advanced software stack or enterprise platform only succeeds when aligned with the people who build, manage, and use it. That is why we prioritize people-skills, motivation, domain awareness, and client orientation right alongside deep technical mastery.

Whether deploying a custom application through our 4D methodology or placing vetted specialists on contract, we integrate seamlessly with your corporate culture, legacy systems, and strategic objectives.

Proprietary Operational Framework

The S3R℠ Approach

Integrated across all NIC Infotek engagements, the S3R℠ process is engineered to Simplify complex IT landscapes while simultaneously driving 3 Reductions that matter most to executive leadership:

R1 · Risk Reduce Operational Risk Proven governance, seamless legacy compatibility, and thoroughly vetted domain specialists.
R2 · Time Reduce Time-to-Market Rapid deployment, iterative release cycles, and 24/7 global delivery velocity.
R3 · Cost Reduce Total IT Cost Blended onsite, offsite, and offshore economics that cut fixed overhead and maximize ROI.
